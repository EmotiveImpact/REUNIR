# REUNIR Product v0.1

## Thesis

REUNIR is an operating system for communities that **learn and do things together**.

It combines:

- **Community** — people, Spaces, feed, conversation and events
- **Learning** — Tracks, lessons, cohorts and progress
- **Doing** — Missions, Projects, Proof and Opportunities

## Core loop

> Discover → Learn → Do → Share proof → Get feedback → Collaborate → Progress → Unlock opportunity.

## First customer

**Code Black** is tenant #001 and should exercise the full product rather than receive a bespoke fork.

## Design principle

The member experience should feel like a living network, not a reporting dashboard. The first screen should answer what is happening, what matters now, who is involved and what the member can do next.
